Prototype Chosen Mathematical Calculation:
DHI = 100 × [ 0.40 × min(AvgSleep / TargetSleep, 1.2) + 0.35 × min(AvgSteps / TargetSteps, 1.2) + 0.25 × min(AvgWater / TargetWater, 1.2) ]
  • Normalized against faculty-set weekly department targets.
  • Each metric is capped at 120% (1.20) so extreme outliers cannot distort the score.
  • Missing records are excluded from denominators (never treated as 0 hours).
  • Private health entries are kept confidential and aggregated at department level.
