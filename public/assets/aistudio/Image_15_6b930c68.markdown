## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a clinical, supportive, and privacy-conscious interface engineered specifically for demanding academic environments. Balancing institutional rigor with psychological calm, the visual environment avoids patronizing gamification, infantilizing illustrations, and disruptive visual noise. 

The aesthetic is grounded in modern executive software: disciplined typography, generous negative space, crisp structural lines, and deep navy anchors. The emotional objective is cognitive decompression—providing students in high-stress technical disciplines with a focused, confidential sanctuary to log metrics, analyze behavioral patterns, and access institutional health resources without sensory fatigue.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-order contrast anchored by deep naval hues, cool atmospheric surfaces, and precise diagnostic accents. 

### Core Palette
- **Canvas Base**: `#F5F8FD` provides a calming, anti-glare canvas that recedes behind active content.
- **Card Surfaces**: `#FFFFFF` provides clean foreground isolation for modules and data visualizers.
- **Structural Chrome / Sidebar**: `#12254A` delivers grounding authority and clear visual hierarchy for primary navigation zones. Active state highlights utilize `#1E3A6E` or `#3B6FD8` with controlled opacity.
- **Primary Interactive**: `#3B6FD8` serves as the primary action driver (buttons, focused strokes, active indicators), transitioning to `#2D5BB8` on hover.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Headings / Emphasized**: `#12254A` ensuring crisp contrast ratios well above accessibility standards.
- **Body & Secondary Text**: `#64748B` (Slate) maintains effortless legibility without high-contrast visual vibration.
- **Muted & Metadata**: `#94A3B8` for supportive captions, timestamps, and placeholder cues.
- **Dividers & Surface Outlines**: `#E6ECF5` creates quiet structural boundaries.

### Diagnostic Accents
Reserved exclusively for data visualization, trend line graphs, and status chips:
- **Sleep Health**: `#8B7FD8` / Deep: `#7C68DC`
- **Hydration**: `#38BDF8` / Deep: `#0284C7`
- **Academic Focus / Study**: `#10B981` / Deep: `#059669`
- **State of Mind / Mood**: `#F59E0B` / Deep: `#F97316`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scannability and high structural legibility. Monospaced or characterful display fonts are avoided in favor of a clean, systematic sans-serif (`Inter`). 

- Headings utilize balanced negative letter-spacing to reinforce structure without feeling heavy.
- Body weights are locked to Regular (400) and Medium (500) to maintain crisp character rendering on high-density monitors.
- Uppercase text transformations are strictly reserved for low-level metadata labels and small badges; they are never applied to buttons or section headings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an exact 8px base rhythm, optimizing horizontal flow through a fluid dashboard grid.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: Multi-column responsive layout anchored by a fixed 260px vertical sidebar. Content runs on a 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: Sidebar shifts to an off-canvas drawer or condensed navigation rail (72px). Grid condenses to 8 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: Single-column linear flow with `1rem` (16px) outer canvas margins. Card padding condenses from `2rem` to `1rem`.

Card interior padding is standardized at `1.5rem` (24px) for metric widgets and `2rem` (32px) for detailed analytical modules and clinical check-in flows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through structural layering rather than dramatic elevation steps. The platform employs restrained, dual-layer ambient shadows tinted with the primary navy tone to maintain cohesive atmospheric warmth.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F5F8FD` with no depth or shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Panels)**: `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a continuous `1px` border of `#E6ECF5` and a twin-pass ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(18, 37, 74, 0.05), 0 6px 16px rgba(18, 37, 74, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Select Menus, Flyouts)**: Elevated white surface with a `1px` border of `#E6ECF5` and deep ambient isolation: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(18, 37, 74, 0.06), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(18, 37, 74, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Crisis Resources)**: Centered display over an overlay scrim of `rgba(18, 37, 74, 0.45)` with a backdrop blur of `4px` to eliminate background distractions.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines professional composure with approachable, ergonomic contours. Standard modules and surfaces embrace gentle corner radii, avoiding razor-sharp corporate edges while rejecting informal bubble forms.

- **Panels & Cards**: 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg` override for high-order containers).
- **Primary & Secondary Buttons**: 12px radius, offering a modern, tactile touchpoint.
- **Form Controls & Search**: 10px radius for balanced, comfortable inputs.
- **Badges, Metric Chips & Avatars**: Continuous full pill radius (`9999px`) to distinguish interactive inline chips from structural containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#3B6FD8` fill, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), 12px border radius, 44px height. Hover: `#2D5BB8`. Active: scale transform down to `0.985`. Focus ring: 3px `#3B6FD8` at 25% opacity with 2px white offset.
- **Secondary / Outline**: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `#E6ECF5`, `#12254A` text. Hover: `#F5F8FD` background with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent fill, `#64748B` text. Hover: `#F5F8FD` background, `#12254A` text.

### Metric Chips & Badges
- 28px height, rounded-full geometry, 8px horizontal padding.
- Consists of a tinted 10% opacity background matched with its solid diagnostic accent color for text and indicator icons (e.g., Sleep: `#8B7FD8` text on `rgba(139, 127, 216, 0.10)`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Height: 44px. Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `#E6ECF5`. Border radius: 10px.
- Text: `#12254A` (`body-md`), Placeholder: `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border changes to `#3B6FD8` with a 3px outer ring in `rgba(59, 111, 216, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Modules
- Solid `#FFFFFF` fill, 16px corner radius, 1px `#E6ECF5` perimeter border.
- Internal padding spans 24px to 32px. Card headers feature 16px semibold titles in `#12254A` paired with subtle secondary actions or contextual badges.

### Lists & Activity Logs
- Clean row layout with 1px border-bottom divider (`#E6ECF5`), zero background noise.
- Hover states transition to subtle `#F5F8FD` backgrounds across interactive list rows.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- 20px box dimensions with 6px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `#CBD5E1` on `#FFFFFF` ground.
- Checked: Solid `#3B6FD8` fill with crisp white geometric check/dot indicators.

### Iconography & Graphics
- Iconography is strictly functional, utilizing single-weight line icons (2px stroke, outline style, 20px / 24px bounding box). 
- Avoid all expressive emojis, hand-drawn flourishes, and illustrative corporate cartoons.
